Discover Taganga

A village with beach soul.

Taganga is the gateway to some of the most beautiful beaches in the Colombian Caribbean. Just 15 minutes from Santa Marta, it combines local life, nature and tranquility.

Must-visit beaches

The main one

Taganga Beach

Dark sand beach with calm waters. Perfect for swimming, snorkeling and enjoying beach life without leaving the village.

In Taganga · by boat or on foot

Playa Grande

One of the most beautiful beaches in the area. Turquoise water, white sand and excellent conditions for snorkeling and diving. Part of Taganga.

Hidden

Bahía Concha

A hidden gem accessible by trail or boat. Crystal-clear waters and the perfect setting to disconnect.

Things to do

Activities in Taganga.

More than just beaches: there are trails, adventure and local culture to discover.

🤿

Diving & snorkeling

Taganga is one of the best diving spots in the Colombian Caribbean. Certified schools take you to reefs and underwater formations.

🥾

Hiking

Walk to Playa Grande, hike up Cerro de la Popa and take trails to Tayrona National Park with panoramic views.

🌅

Sunsets

Sunsets from Taganga Bay are legendary. Fishing boats, hills and a sky that changes color every afternoon.

🐟

Artisanal fishing

Watch local fishermen head out at dawn. You can join them on a sunrise fishing trip.

🐢

Tayrona Park

Just 20 minutes by boat. Virgin beaches, tropical jungle and Colombia's most famous beach: Cabo San Juan.

🍹

Nightlife

Beachfront bars, live music and quiet nights. Taganga has its own rhythm, without the city noise.

Practical info

How to get there and where to stay.

How to get there

  • By air: Simón Bolívar Airport (SMR), then taxi or bus 30-40 min.
  • By bus: From Santa Marta, local buses and taxis connect Santa Marta with Taganga; schedules can vary.
  • By boat: Boat options depend on the destination and current maritime operations; confirm the service before traveling.
  • By motorcycle: Rent one and explore at your own pace. We recommend T&T Rent Motos.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about Taganga that help you plan your trip.

Where is Taganga, Colombia?

Taganga is a coastal district of Santa Marta, in Magdalena, on Colombia’s Caribbean coast, just north of central Santa Marta.

Is Taganga worth visiting?

Taganga can be a good fit for travelers interested in beaches, diving, nature, sunsets and coastal excursions, as well as its proximity to Santa Marta and Tayrona.

Is Taganga safe for tourists?

Safety can vary by area and time. As with any tourist destination, keep personal belongings secure, avoid poorly traveled areas at night and follow current local recommendations.

What is Taganga known for?

Taganga is known for its fishing-village setting, beaches, diving, snorkeling, boat trips and access to nearby coastal and natural attractions.

What to do in Taganga, Colombia?

Popular activities include visiting beaches, snorkeling or diving, walking to Playa Grande, watching the sunset, trying local restaurants and arranging boat excursions.

What beaches are near Taganga?

Taganga Beach is in the village and Playa Grande can be reached from Taganga on foot or by boat. Other beaches and bays can be reached by land or sea from Taganga and Santa Marta.

Is Playa Grande in Taganga?

Playa Grande is closely associated with Taganga and is commonly visited from the village by walking or boat. Travel time depends on the route and sea conditions.

How do you get from Taganga to Tayrona National Park?

Tayrona can be reached by authorized boat services from Taganga to certain sectors, or by traveling overland to one of the park entrances. The best route depends on the area of the park you want to visit.

Can you get from Taganga to Tayrona by boat?

Yes. Colombia’s National Natural Parks authority recognizes maritime access to Tayrona from Taganga. Available sectors and service conditions can vary, so confirm operation before traveling.

How do you get from Taganga to Santa Marta?

Taganga is connected to Santa Marta by road. You can use local public transport or a taxi; travel time depends on traffic and your exact destination.

How do you get from Taganga to Minca?

The usual route combines transport between Taganga and Santa Marta with onward transport to Minca. Travel time and cost depend on the transport option and connections available that day.

How do you get from Taganga to Cartagena?

There is no single direct option that is always the most convenient. A common approach is to travel overland from Santa Marta and continue to Cartagena; compare schedules and prices for your travel date.

How do you get to Taganga from Santa Marta airport?

From Simón Bolívar International Airport in Santa Marta, continue by taxi or ground transport to Taganga. Travel time depends on traffic and road conditions.

What to do in Taganga in one day?

You can combine a morning at the beach or a sea activity, lunch by the water, an afternoon excursion or walk, and sunset in the bay before returning to rest.

Does Taganga have nightlife?

Yes. Taganga has bars and restaurants with evening atmosphere, although its nightlife is smaller and more relaxed than the broader nightlife scene in Santa Marta.

What is the best time to visit Taganga?

Taganga can be visited throughout the year. Weather, sea conditions, tourism season and activity schedules can vary, so check current conditions for your dates.
