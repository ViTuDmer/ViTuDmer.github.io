Frequently asked questions about Casa D'mer and Taganga.

Where is Casa D'mer?

We are at Calle 9 #1-33, Taganga, Santa Marta, near the end of the boardwalk and around 40 steps from Taganga Beach.

Which services are available?

There is a pool, green patio, hammock terrace, kitchen access and Wi-Fi. Some rooms have air conditioning; please confirm when booking.

How do I book direct?

Choose your dates, nights and guests on this page. We will take you to WhatsApp to confirm availability and the best room for you.

Is it a good fit for couples and longer stays?

Yes. Casa D'mer combines a near-sea location, restful spaces and value for relaxed trips of several days.
