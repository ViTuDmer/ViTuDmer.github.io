Discover Santa Marta

The city you did not expect.

Santa Marta is so much more than a stopover. Its historic center, bay and nightlife are worth a visit. Just 15 minutes from Taganga, you get a full day of beach, food and culture before sleeping 40 steps from the sea.

Neighborhoods you cannot miss

Food and art

Historic Center

Colonial houses, free museums and the best food in the city. Streets to walk, drink coffee and discover flavors.

Life and sunsets

Callejon del Correo and Parque de los Novios

The social heart of Santa Marta. Bars, restaurants and live music. The perfect place to end the day with a drink.

Beach and sea

Santa Marta Bay and Rodadero

Boardwalk, sunsets over the sea and Rodadero 15 min away with beach, fresh seafood and beach life.

Things to do

A full day from Taganga.

More than city: beaches, nature and adventure to discover.

🏖

Playa Blanca

On Baru Island. Boat from Rodadero, 45 minutes. White sand, turquoise water and fish lunch by the sea.

🐢

Tayrona

Direct boat from Taganga, 20 minutes. Cabo San Juan, La Piscina, trails and virgin beaches. Early departure, sunset return.

☕

Minca

Waterfalls, coffee farms and Sierra Nevada views. Bus 45 minutes from Santa Marta. A day to break the beach routine.

🏛

Gold Museum and Quinta de San Pedro

Free Tairona culture downtown. The house where Bolivar died, botanical garden and art on the city outskirts.

🌅

Sunset tour

Boats from Rodadero or Taganga. Cocktails, music and frequent dolphins. Two hours watching the city from the sea.

🤿

Bahia Concha

Boat 15 minutes from Taganga. Crystal waters, snorkeling and few people. Perfect for a day of total disconnection.

Practical info

How to get there and back.

How to get there

  • By bus: Every 15 minutes from Taganga entrance. 15-20 min, $3,000 COP.
  • By taxi: 15 minutes, $25,000-35,000 COP.
  • By boat: From Taganga to Tayrona or Bahia Concha.
  • By motorcycle: Rent one and explore at your own pace. We recommend T&T Rent Motos.

Come back to rest

After your day in Santa Marta, come back to Taganga to sleep 40 steps from the sea. Casa Dmer has a pool, hammock terrace and kitchen to prepare something light.

Frequently asked questions

Santa Marta from Taganga.

How do I get to Santa Marta?

Buses every 15 min from Taganga entrance ($3,000 COP, 15-20 min) or taxi ($25,000-35,000 COP).

What beaches can I visit?

Playa Blanca, Bahia Concha, Rodadero and Tayrona. All are full-day excursions.

Where to eat well?

Historic Center has the best food. Callejon del Correo and Parque de los Novios for atmosphere. Rodadero for seafood.

Is it worth going from Taganga?

Yes. 15 min by bus you have city, gastronomy and beaches. You sleep 40 steps from the sea.
