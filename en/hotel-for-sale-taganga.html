Questions about the property for sale.

The property is located at Calle 9 #1-33, Taganga, Santa Marta, Magdalena, Colombia, approximately 40 meters from the beach.

Yes. The property includes sea-view spaces, including a large terrace on the second floor and a balcony with a view on the third floor.
